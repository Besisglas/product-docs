# Troubleshooting

## Cannot identify the product?

Check the label and packaging, then compare the [product directory](../products/index.md). TH01 has separate elongated AAA, round and LCD guides.

## Cannot pair or reconnect?

Record your exact device variant, hub or coordinator, integration and software version. Note the indicator behavior and any error message. Follow the supplied manual for your device; a verified model-specific reset sequence is not yet available here.

## Readings or events seem unexpected?

Record what you expected, what appeared, and when it happened. Include a screenshot and describe where the device is installed. Verified measurement ranges, reporting intervals and placement guidance will be added to the relevant product guide.

## Still need help?

Use the [support checklist](../support/index.md) to prepare your request. Detailed troubleshooting procedures are being prepared.
