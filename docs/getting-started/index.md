# Getting Started

## 1. Identify your product

Check the model on the device or packaging, then open its [product page](../products/index.md). For TH01, also match the elongated, round or LCD design.

## 2. Check the supplied instructions

Use the instructions included with your exact variant. Battery type, installation method and reset sequence must be confirmed for that device.

## 3. Choose your setup path

For a Zigbee device, identify which Zigbee integration your system uses. The pages below are entry points for forthcoming guides; listing a platform here does not confirm compatibility with every product.

- [Zigbee2MQTT](zigbee2mqtt.md)
- [Home Assistant ZHA](zha.md)
- [Pairing & Reset](pairing-reset.md)

For a 433 MHz receiver, use its [product page](../products/433-receiver/index.md); the Zigbee setup path does not apply.

See [Support](../support/index.md) for the details to include with your question.
